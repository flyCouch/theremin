PROJECT: Tim's Theremin (Lyttle reSearch)
REFERENCE: 432 Hz Natural Tuning
STORAGE: 2MB Internal Flash (LittleFS)
EDITOR: XED

================================================================

PERFORMANCE CONTROLS (JOYSTICKS)
================================================================

X-AXIS (Horizontal): Logarithmic Pitch

Purpose: Silent frequency selection.

Visual: Target frequency (Hz) appears on OLED as a 'Ghost'.

Scaling: Logarithmic curve centered on 432 Hz.

Y-AXIS (Vertical): Logarithmic Volume

+Y (Up): Clean signal swell (Logarithmic).

-Y (Down): Effected signal swell (Volume + Preset Grit/Effect).

Center (0): Deadzone / Total Silence.

ULTRASOUND SENSOR: Wah/Resonance

Purpose: Proximity-based filter control (Q-Factor).

Interaction: Physical distance modulates the 'throatiness' of the sound.

================================================================
2. THE SWITCH MATRIX (4x4 KEYPAD)
TOP ZONE: PRESET MANAGEMENT
Buttons 1, 2, 5, 6: Presets for LEFT Joystick
Buttons 3, 4, 7, 8: Presets for RIGHT Joystick

LONG PRESS: Save current RAM state to internal Flash.
                     Ram is default or the last saved state,

BOTTOM ZONE: CONSTRUCTION & ISLANDS
Switch 9 (Left) / 11 (Right): OSCILLATOR BUILDER

CLICK 9,11: Cycle icon (Sine/Square/Tri/Saw) and HIGHLIGHT next slot.

QUICK CLICK 13, 15: Adds whatever the icon is

LONG PRESS 13, 15 (2s): REMOVE the currently highlighted oscillator.

Switch 10 (Left) / 12 (Right): ISLAND SELECTOR

CLICK 10, 12: Cycle focused effect: FILTER -> PAN -> CHORUS -> REVERB -> DELAY.

QUICK CLICK 14, 16: Adds whatever the icon is.

LONG PRESS 14, 16: removes whatever the icon is.

================================================================
3. VISUAL FEEDBACK (OLED & 1602)
OLED NODE MAP:

GHOST ICON: Outline only. Represents a staged change.

SOLID ICON: Filled shape. Represents active audio worker.

HIGHLIGHT: Inverted color block. Shows which node is being edited.

1602 LCD MONITOR:

LINE 1: [Frequency 1 Hz] | [Frequency 2 Hz]

LINE 2: [editing info]

================================================================
4. TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS (FOR CODE EXPORTS)
BAUDRATE: 115200 for Serial Monitor.

HEADER: Must include FILE, DATE, and TIME.

PROTOCOL: Employer (Core 0) / Worker (Core 1).
